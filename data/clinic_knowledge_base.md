# Clinic Knowledge Base

Staff: edit this file directly. It is loaded into the agent's instructions at
the start of every call, so answers to these topics come from here rather
than the model guessing. Keep it short — this is meant for quick facts, not a
full policy manual.

## Location

123 Main Street, Suite 200, Springfield.
Parking is available in the lot behind the building.

## Pricing & memberships

- New patient visit: starts at $150, varies by service.
- Membership plan: $39/month, includes two cleanings/check-ups per year and a
  member discount on other services.
- We accept most major insurance plans; ask the caller to have their card
  ready if they want us to check coverage.

## Cancellation / rescheduling policy

Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment.
Late cancellations may be subject to a fee at staff discretion.

## Common non-clinical FAQs

- We offer both in-person and virtual visits for select services.
- Paperwork can be completed online before the visit through the patient portal link sent by email.
- Lost portal access: staff can resend the link during business hours.
