Browser microphone demo

Talk to the clinic helper from your browser.

No SIP trunk or phone call setup needed. Your browser microphone joins a LiveKit room. The helper listens with Cartesia STT, reasons with Claude Haiku, and speaks with Cartesia TTS.

Try saying
I want to book an appointment What are your clinic hours? I need medical advice Can someone call me back?
Safety guardrail

This is a helper, not a doctor replacement. It should not diagnose, prescribe, recommend medication, or answer urgent clinical questions.
